Documentation

Cloud Save Sync keeps your Hytale singleplayer worlds in your own Google Drive, so you can continue the same world on your PC, Mac and Steam Deck.

Installation

  1. Download CloudSaveSync-<version>.jar from the releases page.
  2. Put it in Hytale's Mods folder:
    Windows%APPDATA%\Hytale\UserData\Mods
    macOS~/Library/Application Support/Hytale/UserData/Mods
    Linux~/.local/share/Hytale/UserData/Mods
    Steam Deck~/.var/app/<app id>/data/Hytale/UserData/Mods
  3. When you create or open a world, make sure CloudSaveSync is enabled in its mod list.
Steam Deck: switch to Desktop Mode, open Konsole and run find ~ -type d -path '*Hytale/UserData' 2>/dev/null to find the folder. In the Dolphin file manager, press Ctrl+H to show hidden folders. Create the Mods folder if it doesn't exist.

Install the mod on every device you want to sync. It needs Hytale server version 0.7.x.

First device

  1. Open a world.
  2. Type /cloudsync login and follow the instructions in chat (see login).
  3. Type /cloudsync enable. The world is uploaded right away.

That's it: the world is now uploaded every few minutes while you play and when you leave it. Repeat /cloudsync enable in every world you want to sync.

Other devices

  1. Install the mod and open any world. A new, empty world is fine.
  2. Type /cloudsync login and sign in with the same Google account.
  3. Type /cloudsync list, then /cloudsync download with the number of the world.
  4. Go back to the main menu. The world is in your world list (restart the game if it isn't).

Downloaded worlds are already synced. Open them and play as usual.

Commands

All commands start with /cloudsync. In a world opened to friends, only the world's owner can use them.

/cloudsync login

Signs you in to Google Drive. Do this once per device. Chat shows a link and a short code: open the link on your phone or computer, enter the code and allow access. On computers with browser sign-in available, a browser window opens instead.

/cloudsync login
[Cloud Sync] On your phone or computer, go to https://www.google.com/device and enter the code
[Cloud Sync] ABCD-EFGH
[Cloud Sync] The code works for 30 minutes. This chat will tell you when you're signed in.
[Cloud Sync] Signed in as you@gmail.com.

Signing in doesn't start syncing anything: use enable for each world.

/cloudsync code

The same as login, but always uses a code. Use it if a browser window doesn't open.

/cloudsync code
[Cloud Sync] Getting a sign-in code...
[Cloud Sync] On your phone or computer, go to https://www.google.com/device and enter the code
[Cloud Sync] ABCD-EFGH

/cloudsync logout

Signs out on this device and asks Google to remove the mod's access. Your worlds in Google Drive stay there. Nothing syncs until you sign in again.

/cloudsync logout
[Cloud Sync] Signed out on this device. Your cloud saves stay in your Google Drive.

/cloudsync enable

Starts syncing the world you're in with the Google account you're signed in with, and uploads it right away.

/cloudsync enable
[Cloud Sync] This world is now synced with your Google Drive. Uploading it now...
[Cloud Sync] Backed up to Google Drive (88 MB).

If your Google Drive already has a world with the same name, nothing is overwritten. You're asked to choose a version (see Conflicts).

/cloudsync disable

Stops syncing this world on this device. Its copies in Google Drive stay there and your other devices aren't affected.

/cloudsync disable
[Cloud Sync] 'My Survival' won't sync on this device any more. Its copy in your Google Drive stays there. Type /cloudsync enable to sync it again.

/cloudsync list

Lists your worlds in Google Drive with a number for each, where they are, and when and where they were last saved.

/cloudsync list
[Cloud Sync] Your worlds in Google Drive:
[Cloud Sync] [1] My Survival - this world, saved 5 min ago on GAMING-PC, 88 MB
[Cloud Sync] [2] Creative Build - not on this device, saved 2 hours ago on Steam Deck, 120 MB
[Cloud Sync] To download, type /cloudsync download and the numbers, e.g. /cloudsync download 2 or /cloudsync download 1,3

/cloudsync download

Downloads worlds by their number in /cloudsync list. They appear in your world list when you go back to the main menu.

/cloudsync download 2
[Cloud Sync] Downloading 'Creative Build' (120 MB)...
[Cloud Sync] Added 'Creative Build' (saved 2 hours ago on Steam Deck).
[Cloud Sync] It will be in your world list when you go back to the main menu. If not, restart the game.

More examples:

/cloudsync download 1,3worlds 1 and 3
/cloudsync download allevery cloud world that isn't on this device

A world that already exists here with the same name is never overwritten.

/cloudsync upload

Backs up the world you're in now, without waiting for the next automatic upload.

/cloudsync upload
[Cloud Sync] Uploading this world...
[Cloud Sync] Uploading... 50%
[Cloud Sync] Backed up to Google Drive (88 MB).

/cloudsync status

Shows the account you're signed in with, when this world was last synced, how uploads are set up, how the last upload after leaving went, and the newest version in the cloud.

/cloudsync status
[Cloud Sync] World 'My Survival' on GAMING-PC. Signed in to Google Drive as you@gmail.com.
[Cloud Sync] Last synced 3 min ago (cloud revision 12).
[Cloud Sync] Changes are uploaded every 7 minutes and when you leave the world (/cloudsync auto to change).
[Cloud Sync] Type /cloudsync list to see all your worlds in Google Drive.
[Cloud Sync] Last upload after leaving: done - uploaded as cloud revision 11 (2 hours ago).
[Cloud Sync] Cloud: current version from GAMING-PC, 3 min ago (88 MB); 5 versions kept.

/cloudsync auto

Controls uploads while you play. Applies to all worlds on this device. The upload when you leave a world always happens.

/cloudsync auto 15upload every 15 minutes (1–240, default 7)
/cloudsync auto offdon't upload while playing
/cloudsync auto onturn it back on
/cloudsync auto 15
[Cloud Sync] Uploads while playing: every 15 minutes (all worlds on this device). Your world is also uploaded when you leave it.

/cloudsync keep

How many copies of this world Google Drive keeps. The setting is saved with the world, so all your devices follow it. Lowering it deletes the oldest copies right away.

/cloudsync keepshow the setting and the space used
/cloudsync keep 3keep the 3 newest copies (1–20, default 5)
/cloudsync keep 3
[Cloud Sync] Google Drive will keep the 3 newest copies of 'My Survival' (for all your devices). Removed 2 older copies (176 MB).

/cloudsync usecloud / keeplocal

Used after a conflict, when you're asked which version of a world to keep.

/cloudsync usecloudswitch to the version in Google Drive. Quit to the menu and reopen the world to apply it. This device's version is backed up first.
/cloudsync keeplocalkeep this device's version and upload it as the current one. The cloud version stays in your cloud history.

How it works

Opening a synced world

Before the world loads, the mod checks Google Drive for a newer version (for at most 10 seconds). If there is one, and this device has no changes that were never uploaded, it's downloaded and replaces your local copy, with progress on the loading screen. Your local copy is backed up first. If the download takes longer than a minute, it finishes in the background and is applied the next time you open the world.

While you play

The world is uploaded every 7 minutes (see auto), starting about 2 minutes after you join. Uploads run at low priority, read the save at a limited speed and skip compressing the large region files, so they don't make the game stutter.

When you leave

Hytale closes its world server a few seconds after you quit, which is too short for an upload. The mod starts a small helper process that waits for the game to close, then zips the world and uploads it. It uses the game's own Java, so nothing extra is installed.

What is synced

The world's whole save folder, except logs, telemetry, Hytale's own backup folder and the mod's own data. When a newer version is applied, the world and player data are replaced; the world's settings files stay as they are on your device.

Mods

Every upload records the mods enabled for the world, with their exact versions. When you open the world on another device, missing mods are installed before the world loads, enabled for the world and loaded into the same session, so items and blocks from mods are never lost.

  • Mods are downloaded from CurseForge, the same files the game's mod browser installs, and checked against the checksum recorded on your other device.
  • A mod installed by hand (not from the mod browser) can't be downloaded: install it yourself.
  • A world never opens with mods missing. If one can't be installed, the game goes back to the menu and tells you which.
  • download installs a world's mods together with the world.

Several devices

Each device remembers which cloud version its copy of a world matches, and marks the world as changed while you play. That mark is only cleared after Google confirms an upload. This is how the mod knows when a newer cloud version can safely replace your copy, and when to ask you instead.

If a world was recently open on another device, you see a warning when you open it:

[Cloud Sync] This world was last seen open on Steam Deck 2 min ago. If it's still open there, close it there first, or your changes there and here will conflict.

For the smoothest experience, quit the world on one device before opening it on another.

Conflicts

A conflict happens when Google Drive has a newer version of a world and this device has changes that were never uploaded, for example after playing the same world offline on two devices. The mod never overwrites anything in that case:

[Cloud Sync] Your Google Drive has a newer version of this world, a version from Steam Deck (1 hour ago), and this device has changes that were never uploaded. Nothing was overwritten.
[Cloud Sync] Type /cloudsync usecloud to switch to the cloud version (this one is backed up), or /cloudsync keeplocal to keep this one (the cloud one stays in the history).

Until you choose, you play this device's version and it isn't uploaded over the cloud version. Whichever you choose, the other version is kept: as a local backup with usecloud, or as a pinned copy in Google Drive with keeplocal.

Playing offline

Without an internet connection, the world loads from your device as usual and you see:

[Cloud Sync] No connection to Google Drive; playing your local copy. It will sync when you're back online.

Nothing is lost. The next time you play that world online, your progress is uploaded, and if another device uploaded a newer version in the meantime, you're asked which one to keep.

Google accounts

A world syncs with the account that was signed in when you used /cloudsync enable. If you sign in with a different account, nothing syncs with it until you enable worlds for it. Use the same account on all your devices.

Copies and space

Each upload creates a copy in Google Drive. The 5 newest copies of each world are kept by default (see keep); older ones are deleted automatically. Versions set aside in a conflict are kept separately (up to 5 per world). A world usually takes 50–150 MB per copy.

Where files are

Google Drivea folder called Hytale Cloud Saves
Sign-inUserData/CloudSaveSync/google-account.json
SettingsUserData/CloudSaveSync/settings.json
Local backupsUserData/CloudSaveSync/backups/<world> (newest 10 per world)
Upload logUserData/CloudSaveSync/worlds/<world>/helper.log

UserData is the folder that contains Hytale's Mods and Saves folders.

Privacy

  • Your worlds are stored in your own Google Drive and nowhere else.
  • The mod can only see files it created itself, never anything else in your Drive.
  • There is no developer server. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone other than Google.

Read the full privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

No /cloudsync commandCheck the jar is in UserData/Mods and CloudSaveSync is enabled in the world's mod list.
"This world isn't synced"Type /cloudsync enable.
"No connection to Google Drive"Check your internet connection. Your world uploads later.
"You've been signed out"Type /cloudsync login again.
"Your Google Drive is full"Free some space or keep fewer copies with /cloudsync keep.
Downloaded world not in the menuRestart the game.
"The cloud upload didn't finish"It's retried while you play. See the upload log under Where files are.

Still stuck? Report a problem with the output of /cloudsync status and your system (Windows, macOS, Linux or Steam Deck).

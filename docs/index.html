Cloud Save Sync

A Hytale mod that syncs your singleplayer worlds between your PC, Mac and Steam Deck through your own Google Drive.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google once per device. The mod can only see the files it creates in your Drive.
  • Choose which worlds to sync. They are uploaded while you play and when you leave the world.
  • When you open a synced world, the newest version from your other devices is loaded first.
  • If two devices changed the same world, nothing is overwritten: you choose which version to keep.
  • Your worlds stay in your own Google Drive. There is no server in between.

Read the documentation for every command with examples, and guides for several devices, conflicts and offline play.

Getting started

  1. Put the mod's .jar file in Hytale's UserData/Mods folder.
  2. Open a world and type /cloudsync login, then follow the instructions in chat.
  3. Type /cloudsync enable to start syncing that world.
  4. On another device, type /cloudsync list and /cloudsync download <number> to get your worlds there.

Commands

/cloudsync loginSign in to Google Drive
/cloudsync enable / disableStart or stop syncing this world
/cloudsync listShow your worlds in Google Drive
/cloudsync download 1,3Download worlds to this device
/cloudsync uploadBack up this world now
/cloudsync statusShow the sync status
/cloudsync auto on|off|<minutes>Uploads while playing
/cloudsync keep <1-20>How many copies of this world to keep
/cloudsync usecloud / keeplocalChoose a version after a conflict
/cloudsync logoutSign out on this device

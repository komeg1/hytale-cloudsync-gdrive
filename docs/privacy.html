Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Cloud Save Sync is a free, open-source mod for Hytale that copies your singleplayer worlds to your own Google Drive so you can continue playing them on your other devices. This page explains what data the mod uses and what it does with it.

The short version

  • Your worlds go to your Google Drive and nowhere else.
  • The mod can only see files it created itself. It cannot see, read or change anything else in your Drive.
  • There is no server run by the developer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone other than Google.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

What the mod accesses in your Google account

When you sign in, the mod asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it, the mod can create files in your Google Drive and read, update and delete only the files it created. It does not request access to your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

The files it creates are in a folder called Hytale Cloud Saves in your Drive:

  • zip copies of the worlds you choose to sync (world, player and settings files from your Hytale save folder);
  • a small file per world listing its copies, including the device name and time of each upload;
  • a small file per world noting which device currently has the world open.

What is stored on your device

In Hytale's user data folder (UserData/CloudSaveSync), the mod keeps:

  • the Google sign-in token that lets it upload without asking you to sign in every time;
  • a random device identifier and your computer's name, so your worlds' history shows which device saved them;
  • sync status, settings, logs and local backups of worlds it replaced with a cloud version.

This data stays on your device. The device name also appears in the files in your own Drive, as described above.

Who the data is shared with

The mod only talks to Google (sign-in and Google Drive). The developer receives nothing: there is no server, no telemetry and no crash reporting run by the developer. Your data is not sold or shared with anyone.

Cloud Save Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Type /cloudsync logout in Hytale to sign out. This deletes the sign-in token from your device and asks Google to revoke it.
  • You can also remove the mod's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Hytale Cloud Saves folder in Google Drive to remove your cloud copies.
  • Delete the UserData/CloudSaveSync folder to remove everything the mod stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
